1. Overview & Core Philosophy

SudoBuddy is designed as a logic puzzle game combining Classic Sudoku, Animal Mode, and Murdoku (a logic deduction floor plan mystery game), alongside real-time 1v1 Speed Duels and daily seeded investigations.

Our core privacy philosophy is simple:

  • Play without an account: You can play SudoBuddy completely as a Guest without submitting any email, password, or identifying credentials.
  • No sale of personal data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers. Advertising in the free version is served strictly through Google AdMob, with prior explicit consent obtained for users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK.
  • Transparency: Cloud connectivity (such as online leaderboards and multiplayer relay) operates exclusively to power in-game features.
