Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. This document details how SudoBuddy collects, utilizes, safeguards, and handles personal data in accordance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play Data Safety policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Application: SudoBuddy Package Name: com.rdba.sudobuddy Developer: Ricardo Barbosa Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

SudoBuddy is designed as a logic puzzle game combining Classic Sudoku and Murdoku (a logic deduction floor plan mystery game), alongside real-time 1v1 Speed Duels and daily seeded investigations.

Our core privacy philosophy is simple:

  • Play without an account: You can play SudoBuddy completely as a Guest without submitting any email, password, or identifying credentials.
  • No ad trackers or data brokers: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data to advertisers.
  • Transparency: Any cloud connectivity (such as online leaderboards and multiplayer relay) operates exclusively to power in-game features.

3. Detailed Data Collection & Processing

We process the following categories of data strictly for the purposes described:

A. Authentication & Account Information

  • Google Sign-In: If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication receives your Google User ID, display name, email address, and profile photo URL. This allows your detective rank and leaderboard placements to be preserved across sessions and devices.
  • Detective Codename (Local / Guest): If you do not sign in with Google, you can enter an alias/codename (e.g., "Sherlock") which is stored locally on your device or submitted alongside scores.
  • Guest Mode: Playing as a guest does not require any email or account creation.

B. Leaderboard & Gameplay Records

  • When you complete a Daily Sudoku or Daily Murdoku puzzle while connected to the internet, your solve time (in seconds), date of the challenge, game type, and chosen display name are sent to Google Cloud Firestore to populate today's top detective leaderboard and the Hall of Fame.
  • You may reset or wipe these scores at any time from the app or by requesting account deletion.

C. Real-time Multiplayer (Speed Duel)

  • When participating in a 1v1 Speed Duel, our relay server (Socket.io) pairs two players using a temporary 6-digit room code.
  • During the match, your solve percentage and mistake counter are broadcast in real-time to your opponent so both players can observe each other's progress.
  • No permanent storage: Once the match ends or both players disconnect, all room data in server memory is automatically discarded.

D. Local Device Storage (AsyncStorage)

  • Your puzzle progress, pencil notes, streaks, game settings (color theme mode, vibration/haptics toggle, notes-by-default, language preference) are saved directly in local flash storage on your device.
  • This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted across the network unless you explicitly submit a solved score.

5. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

Security: All communication between SudoBuddy and cloud services utilizes modern TLS/HTTPS and Secure WebSockets (WSS) encryption. Data stored in Google Cloud Firestore is protected by strict security rules prohibiting unauthorized access.

Retention Period:

  • Account credentials and leaderboard scores: Retained for as long as your account remains active or until you request deletion.
  • Multiplayer session data: Discarded immediately upon conclusion of the match session.
  • Local puzzle history: Retained on device until you choose to reset stats in the Settings screen or clear app storage.

Account & Data Deletion: You have the absolute right to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time. You can do this either directly within the app (Settings → Delete Account) or via our dedicated web resource:

Open Account & Data Deletion Page →

6. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Depending on your location, you hold the following statutory data protection rights:

  • Right of Access: You can request a confirmation of the personal data held about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can change your Detective Codename directly inside the app settings at any time.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can request the permanent deletion of your account and leaderboard scores.
  • Right to Restriction / Objection: You can choose to play offline or strictly in Guest mode without cloud synchronization.

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app settings or submit a request on our Account Deletion Page or via the contact email below.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

SudoBuddy is an intellectual logic and deduction puzzle game designed for general audiences. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or store personal data from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union).

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under the relevant age without parental consent, we will take immediate steps to purge that data from our servers.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, new gameplay capabilities, or evolving legal obligations. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
