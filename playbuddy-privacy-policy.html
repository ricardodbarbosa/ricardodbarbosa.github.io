Compliance & Privacy

Privacy Policy for PlayBuddy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Overview (Offline-First Architecture)

PlayBuddy ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed with a privacy-by-design, offline-first architecture. We respect your privacy and believe your personal data belongs to you.

  • Local Storage: All user game data (player profiles, coins, match records, unlocked items, and achievements) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage.
  • No Account Registration: No sign-up, email registration, or account login is required to play.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

PlayBuddy does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII), including but not limited to:

  • Real name, identity cards, or physical address
  • Email address or phone number
  • Precise or coarse GPS location
  • Contacts, phonebook, microphone, or camera access

3. Multiplayer & Pass 'n Play Privacy

In multiplayer modes requiring secret hands (such as Sueca, UNO, and Battleship), our Fog-of-War / Privacy Shield feature prevents visual snooping on shared screens. For online matches, matchmaking only transfers transient match state (board coordinates, card plays). No persistent tracking profiles are created on game servers.

4. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

PlayBuddy uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve non-intrusive advertisements to support free development. Google may collect device identifiers and diagnostic information in order to provide ads:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID, IDFA)
  • Device model, OS version, and network performance indicators

To learn how Google manages privacy and advertising data, visit:
Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps.

5. Virtual Currency

Any coins, badges, or points earned in PlayBuddy are solely for in-game entertainment and progression. They possess no monetary cash value, cannot be redeemed for fiat currency, and cannot be transferred outside of your local game installation.

6. Children's Privacy

PlayBuddy is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or under the age specified by local laws, such as GDPR / COPPA).

7. Data Control & Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all records at any time by clearing the application storage/cache in your operating system settings or by uninstalling PlayBuddy.
