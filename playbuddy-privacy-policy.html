Google Play Store & GDPR Compliant

Privacy Policy for PlayBuddy

Last updated: November 17, 2026 • Buddy App Studio

1. Overview (Offline-First Architecture)

PlayBuddy ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by Ricardo Barbosa (Buddy App Studio), is designed with a privacy-by-design, offline-first architecture. We respect your privacy and believe your personal data belongs to you.

  • Local Storage: All user game data (player profiles, coins, match records, unlocked items, and achievements) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage.
  • No Account Registration: No sign-up, email registration, or account login is required to play.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

PlayBuddy does not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information (PII), including but not limited to:

  • Real name, identity cards, or physical address
  • Email address or phone number
  • Precise or coarse GPS location
  • Contacts, phonebook, microphone, or camera access

3. Multiplayer & Pass 'n Play Privacy

In multiplayer modes requiring secret hands (such as Sueca, UNO, and Battleship), our Fog-of-War / Privacy Shield feature prevents visual snooping on shared screens. For online matches, matchmaking only transfers transient match state (board coordinates, card plays). No persistent tracking profiles are created on game servers.

4. Advertising, Third-Party SDKs & GDPR Consent

PlayBuddy uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve non-intrusive advertisements to support free development. Google may collect device identifiers and diagnostic information in order to provide ads and prevent fraud:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID) and IP address
  • Device model, OS version, and ad interaction metrics

European Consent (Google UMP SDK): For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK, PlayBuddy obtains prior explicit consent via Google's certified User Messaging Platform (UMP) before initializing ads. You can update or withdraw your ad consent at any time via Settings > Privacy / Ad Preferences inside the App, or reset your advertising ID via your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

To learn how Google manages privacy and advertising data, visit:
Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps.

5. Virtual Currency

Any coins, badges, or points earned in PlayBuddy are solely for in-game entertainment and progression. They possess no monetary cash value, cannot be redeemed for fiat currency, and cannot be transferred outside of your local game installation.

6. Children's Privacy

PlayBuddy is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction, such as 16 in certain EU Member States) and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

7. Data Control, Deletion & GDPR Rights

Because your game data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all records at any time by clearing the application storage/cache in your operating system settings or by uninstalling PlayBuddy.

  • GDPR Legal Bases (Art. 6 GDPR): Local game progress and transient match state are processed strictly to perform the game service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b)), while advertising identifiers via Google AdMob UMP are processed solely based on your explicit prior consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • User Rights & Supervisory Authority: EEA/UK users have the right to access, rectify, erase, or object to the processing of their data, withdraw ad consent in the App settings, and lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority (in Portugal, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados — CNPD at www.cnpd.pt).
