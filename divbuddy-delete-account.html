Google Play Account Deletion Compliance

Account & Data Deletion Request

App: DivBuddy (Expense Splitting & Settlement)
Package: com.rdba.divbuddy
Developer: DivBuddy Team

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, DivBuddy enables users to permanently delete their account and associated personal data at any time, both from directly within the application and via this web resource.

1. How to Delete Your Account In-App (Recommended)

If you currently have the DivBuddy app installed on your device, deleting your account is instant:

  1. Open the DivBuddy app.
  2. Tap the Settings tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Under the Account identity section, tap Delete account.
  4. Review the confirmation dialog. Choose whether you also want to purge offline local data stored on your device.
  5. Tap Permanently Delete Account. If prompted, complete the Google verification step.

Upon confirmation, your Firebase authentication record and cloud profile will be removed immediately from Google servers.

The following table outlines what user data is deleted, what is retained, and the applicable retention period:

Firebase Authentication Account (UID, credentials) Permanently deleted from Firebase Auth. Immediate (0 days)
User Profile (Display name, email mapping, avatar photo URL, push tokens) Permanently deleted from Firestore (users/{uid} and emails/{email}). Immediate (0 days)
Group Invitations All pending invites sent to your email are permanently deleted. Immediate (0 days)
Shared Groups Created/Owned by You The shared group snapshot and membership roster are deleted from Google Cloud Firestore. Immediate (0 days)
Shared Groups Created by Others Your status is set to left and your membership access is revoked. In order to preserve debt settlement integrity for other group members, historical transaction line items remain until the group creator deletes the entire group. Retained in group ledger only to maintain mathematical settlement balance.
Local On-Device Data (SQLite) Stored only on your physical smartphone. You can delete it at any time via Settings > Reset all data or by clearing app storage in device settings. Controlled 100% by you on-device.
Receipt Photos & OCR Data Never uploaded to any server. Processed 100% on-device. 0 days (Never stored on servers)
