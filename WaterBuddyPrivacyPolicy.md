# Privacy Policy for WaterBuddy

**Effective Date:** October 2, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 2, 2026

WaterBuddy ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use the WaterBuddy mobile application (the "App") available on Android (package `com.ricardodbarbosa.waterbuddy`) and iOS.

---

## 1. Summary — At a Glance

- **Offline-First & Local Storage:** Your water logs, hydration history, target goals, and health assessment data are stored locally on your device via AsyncStorage. We do not operate a private database or server tracking your hydration records.
- **No Mandatory Account:** You can use WaterBuddy without creating an account or providing your name.
- **Third-Party Advertising:** The free version uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, ad interaction data, and approximate location.
- **Optional Pro Waitlist:** If you submit your email for the WaterBuddy Pro waitlist, your email is transmitted securely via Web3Forms solely to notify you when the feature is ready.
- **Data Export & Portability:** You have full control over your data and can export or clear your hydration records at any time.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
The following data is created through your use of the App and stored strictly on your device:
- **Hydration Logs:** Timestamps and volume (ml / oz) of water consumed.
- **User Preferences:** Target hydration goal, reminder schedules, theme mode (light/dark), language, and quick-add preset amounts.
- **Personalized Hydration Profile:** Biological sex, weight, activity level, climate type, caffeine intake, and health focus goals (e.g., energy, skin, fitness). This information is used strictly by the on-device algorithm (`healthAnalyzer`) to calculate recommended water intake and actionable hydration insights.
- **Gamification & Rewards:** Coin balances, streak days, and bonus claim timestamps.

*None of the above personal hydration logs or body metric inputs are uploaded to our servers.*

### B. Information Sent Externally Upon Your Action
- **Pro Waitlist Inquiries:** If you voluntarily express interest in WaterBuddy Pro and submit your email address, your email, app language, and coin balance are transmitted via HTTPS to **Web3Forms** (`api.web3forms.com`) to deliver your message to the developer. We do not sell, rent, or use this email address for marketing third-party services.

### C. Automatically Collected Information (Third-Party Services)
The App integrates with third-party software development kits (SDKs) that may collect diagnostic or advertising data:

1. **Google AdMob (react-native-google-mobile-ads):**
   - **Data Collected:** Mobile Advertising IDs (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA), IP address (for approximate location / fraud prevention), ad interaction data, device make/model, OS version, and diagnostic logs.
   - **Purpose:** Serving personalized and non-personalized advertisements (banner, interstitial, rewarded video ads) and preventing advertising fraud.
   - **AdMob Privacy Policy:** [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) | [Google Partner Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

2. **Expo & Crash Telemetry (if applicable):**
   - Standard runtime crash logs and device architecture indicators to maintain app stability.

## 3. Device Permissions

WaterBuddy requests specific device permissions to provide core features:
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** Required to schedule and send timely local reminders so you remember to drink water throughout your active hours. These alarms run locally on the device.
- **Storage / File System / Sharing (`expo-file-system`, `expo-sharing`):** Used when you export or import your hydration history as a JSON backup file.

You can modify or revoke notification permissions at any time through your device system settings.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Data

We use the data collected or stored for the following purposes:
- To calculate and display your daily water intake progress and health coach tips.
- To schedule hydration reminders according to your chosen window and interval.
- To maintain your gamification streak and reward points.
- To display in-app advertisements through Google AdMob to support free app maintenance.
- To notify you about WaterBuddy Pro if you voluntarily joined the waitlist.

## 5. Google Play Health Apps Policy Compliance

In accordance with Google Play's Health Apps and Health-Related Features Policy:
- **No Health Connect Integration:** WaterBuddy does not integrate with, read from, or write to Android Health Connect or Apple HealthKit APIs.
- **On-Device Health & Fitness Processing:** All health-related metrics (water volume consumed, weight, biological sex, activity level, climate, and wellness goals) are entered voluntarily by the user and processed solely on the user's local device for personal habit tracking and algorithm estimation.
- **No Transmission or Sale of Health Data:** We never transmit, sell, broker, or license your hydration logs, body measurements, or health assessment responses to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

## 6. Medical Disclaimer (Non-Medical Purpose)

WaterBuddy is a general wellness, habit-building, and hydration tracking application. **WaterBuddy is NOT a certified medical device and does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or disease prevention.**

- The hydration calculations, hydration score, and actionable tips generated by the in-app AI coach algorithm are general estimations based on user-provided inputs and public dietary hydration recommendations.
- They should not be considered substitutes for clinical judgment, medical advice, diagnosis, or personalized treatment plans prescribed by a qualified healthcare professional.
- Always consult a physician or healthcare specialist if you have pre-existing medical conditions (such as kidney disease, cardiovascular conditions, or electrolyte balance disorders) that require controlled or restricted fluid intake.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Local Data:** All hydration history and settings remain on your device until you:
  - Select **"Reset All Data"** in the App's Settings tab, or
  - Clear the App storage / uninstall the App from your device.
- **Waitlist Email Data:** You may request deletion of your submitted waitlist email by contacting us at the email address provided below.

## 10. Google Play Data Safety & Deletion Requests

In accordance with Google Play's Data Safety and Account/Data Deletion policies:
- **No Mandatory Account Creation:** WaterBuddy does not require account creation, registration, or logins.
- **Local Data Purge:** Users can permanently delete all local hydration records, health assessment metrics, and preferences instantly within the App by navigating to **Settings > Reset Data**.
- **Web/Email Deletion Request:** If you previously submitted your email for the WaterBuddy Pro waitlist and wish to have all traces of your contact email and associated metadata deleted from developer records, you may email **mybuddyappsupport@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request"*. All related records will be permanently purged within 30 days.

## 12. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, or California, USA), you have rights regarding your personal information:
- **Right to Access & Portability:** You can export all your hydration logs at any time via Settings > Export Data (JSON).
- **Right to Erasure:** You can wipe all stored data via Settings > Reset Data.
- **Ad Tracking Choices:** You can manage or reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads via your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads**) or iOS device settings (**Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**).
