# Privacy Policy for WaterBuddy

**Effective Date:** September 4, 2026

WaterBuddy ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the WaterBuddy mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect and Storage
WaterBuddy is designed to minimize data collection. 
* **Local Device Data:** Your hydration goals, intake logs, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your personal hydration logs on external servers.
* **Notifications:** If enabled, notification permissions are used solely to schedule local hydration reminders on your device.

---

### 2. Third-Party Services & Analytics
To support app features, display advertisements, or monetize the application, WaterBuddy may integrate third-party software development kits (SDKs):
* **Google AdMob:** If advertisements are displayed, AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) to serve personalized or non-personalized ads according to Google's privacy policies.

---

### 3. Data Retention and Deletion
Since your hydration tracking data is saved locally on your mobile device, uninstalling the WaterBuddy application or clearing the application data through your device settings will permanently remove all stored app data.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
WaterBuddy does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
