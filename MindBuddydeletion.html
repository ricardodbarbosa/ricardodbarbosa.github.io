MindBuddy — Data Deletion Request

MindBuddy (com.rdba.mindbuddy) is designed with a local-first, privacy-conscious architecture. We do not require accounts to use the app.

1. Local Device Data Deletion

All focus session histories, breathing counts, active streaks, settings, and coins are stored exclusively on your device. You can permanently delete all local data at any time without contacting us:

  • Open your device Settings.
  • Tap Apps > MindBuddy > Storage & cache.
  • Tap Clear Storage / Clear Data, or uninstall the app.

2. Pro Waitlist Email Deletion Request

If you voluntarily submitted your email address to join the MindBuddy Pro early-access waitlist, you have the right to request that your email and associated record be permanently deleted from our mailing list.

To request deletion: Send an email from the address you wish to delete to:

Email mybuddyappsupport@gmail.com

Subject line: MindBuddy Data Deletion Request

We process all data deletion requests within 30 days of receipt, at which point your email record is completely purged.
